# Customer Journey Map
## Overview
The customer journey map for mail-tuner outlines the phases a customer goes through from initial awareness to expansion of the product. The journey is divided into five phases: Aware, Consider, Try, Adopt, and Expand.

### Aware
* **Trigger Event**: Difficulty with email deliverability, high bounce rates, or spam filter issues.
* **Friction Points**: Lack of visibility into email performance, difficulty in configuring email infrastructure.
* **User Emotions**: Frustration, concern about reputation and customer engagement.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Educational content (blog posts, webinars) on email deliverability best practices, highlighting the importance of optimized email infrastructure.
* **Metric**: Website traffic, social media engagement, lead generation (e.g., 500 new visitors, 20 leads).

### Consider
* **Trigger Event**: Realization that email deliverability issues are impacting business performance, search for solutions.
* **Friction Points**: Evaluating competing solutions, concerns about integration and cost.
* **User Emotions**: Hope, skepticism about finding an effective solution.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Free trials, demos, case studies, and customer testimonials showcasing mail-tuner's effectiveness.
* **Metric**: Trial sign-ups (e.g., 50 trials), demo requests (e.g., 10 demos).

### Try
* **Trigger Event**: Decision to trial mail-tuner, initial setup and configuration.
* **Friction Points**: Technical difficulties during onboarding, learning curve for the platform.
* **User Emotions**: Excitement, potential disappointment if expectations are not met.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Seamless onboarding process, responsive customer support, clear documentation.
* **Metric**: Activation rate (e.g., 80% of trial users complete setup), user engagement (e.g., 75% of users log in within the first week).

### Adopt
* **Trigger Event**: Successful trial, decision to subscribe to mail-tuner.
* **Friction Points**: Ongoing technical support needs, potential for feature requests.
* **User Emotions**: Satisfaction, relief that email deliverability issues are being addressed.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Regular software updates, proactive customer support, community engagement.
* **Metric**: Conversion rate from trial to paid (e.g., 40%), customer retention rate (e.g., 90% after 6 months).

### Expand
* **Trigger Event**: Realization of the value mail-tuner provides, consideration of additional features or services.
* **Friction Points**: Evaluating the cost-benefit of upgrading or adding services.
* **User Emotions**: Loyalty, anticipation of continued improvement in email deliverability and business performance.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Upsell and cross-sell opportunities, premium support services, strategic account management.
* **Metric**: Upsell/cross-sell rate (e.g., 20% of customers upgrade), net promoter score (NPS) (e.g., +50).
