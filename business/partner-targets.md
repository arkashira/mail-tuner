## Partner Integration Roadmap for Mail-Tuner

### Target Integrations

| Partner/SaaS/API          | Free-Tier Limits                      | Integration Effort | Value-Add (User Job Solved)                    | Affiliate/Revenue-Share |
|---------------------------|---------------------------------------|--------------------|------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **SendGrid**              | 100 emails/day                        | M                  | Optimize email deliverability and tracking     | Yes                      |
| **Mailgun**               | 5,000 emails/month                    | M                  | Improve email configuration and analytics       | Yes                      |
| **Amazon SES**            | 62,000 emails/month (first year)     | L                  | Cost-effective email sending and deliverability | No                       |
| **Postmark**              | 100 emails/month                      | S                  | Fast email delivery with tracking               | Yes                      |
| **SparkPost**             | 100 emails/day                        | M                  | Advanced analytics for email performance        | Yes                      |
| **Mailchimp**             | 500 contacts, 1,000 emails/month      | M                  | Email marketing integration and performance     | Yes                      |
| **Zoho Mail**             | 5 users, 5GB/user                    | S                  | Seamless email management and deliverability    | No                       |
| **HubSpot**               | 2,000 emails/month (free CRM tier)   | M                  | Integrate email marketing with CRM insights     | Yes                      |

### Rationale for Selection

1. **SendGrid**: A widely used email delivery service that provides robust analytics and deliverability tools. Integrating with SendGrid will allow Mail-Tuner users to optimize their email campaigns and improve delivery rates effectively.

2. **Mailgun**: Known for its powerful email API and analytics, Mailgun offers businesses the ability to manage their email infrastructure efficiently. The integration will help users configure their email settings for better deliverability.

3. **Amazon SES**: A cost-effective solution for sending bulk emails. While it has a higher integration effort, the potential cost savings and scalability make it a valuable addition for businesses looking to optimize their email infrastructure.

4. **Postmark**: Focused on transactional email delivery, Postmark ensures fast delivery and tracking. This integration will help users understand their email performance better and improve engagement rates.

5. **SparkPost**: Offers advanced analytics and insights into email performance. Integrating SparkPost will add value by providing users with data-driven recommendations for improving their email strategies.

6. **Mailchimp**: A popular email marketing platform that allows users to manage their email lists and campaigns. Integrating Mailchimp will help users leverage their marketing efforts alongside deliverability improvements.

7. **Zoho Mail**: A comprehensive email management solution that can enhance the user experience for businesses using Zoho's suite of applications. While it has no affiliate program, it provides a solid email management foundation.

8. **HubSpot**: Integrating with HubSpot will allow users to connect their email marketing efforts with CRM insights, enhancing their overall marketing strategy. The affiliate program adds an extra incentive for collaboration.

### Conclusion

The selected partners provide a mix of email delivery, analytics, and marketing capabilities that align with the Mail-Tuner's goal of optimizing email infrastructure and improving delivery rates. Prioritizing those with affiliate/revenue-share opportunities will also help monetize the integrations effectively.
