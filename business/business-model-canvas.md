# Business Model Canvas for Mail-Tuner

## Customer Segments
- Small to medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) that rely heavily on email marketing.
- E-commerce businesses looking to optimize transactional email deliverability.
- Marketing agencies managing email campaigns for multiple clients.
- IT departments in larger organizations responsible for email infrastructure.

## Value Propositions
- AI-driven insights for optimizing email configurations to improve deliverability rates by up to 30%.
- User-friendly interface that simplifies complex email infrastructure management.
- Real-time monitoring and diagnostics for email performance issues.
- Comprehensive reporting tools to track email deliverability metrics and ROI.

## Channels
- Direct sales through the Mail-Tuner website.
- Partnerships with email service providers (ESPs) for bundled offerings.
- Online marketing campaigns (SEO, PPC) targeting businesses struggling with email deliverability.
- Webinars and workshops to educate potential customers on email optimization.

## Customer Relationships
- Dedicated customer support via chat and email to assist with setup and troubleshooting.
- Regular updates and newsletters providing tips on email best practices.
- Community forums for users to share experiences and solutions.
- Personalized onboarding sessions for enterprise clients.

## Revenue Streams
- Subscription-based pricing model: 
  - Basic Plan: $29/month (1,000 emails) / ~1,000 THB
  - Pro Plan: $99/month (10,000 emails) / ~3,300 THB
  - Enterprise Plan: Custom pricing based on usage.
- One-time setup fee for enterprise clients: $500 / ~17,000 THB.
- Affiliate commissions from partnerships with ESPs.

## Key Resources
- Proprietary AI algorithms for email deliverability optimization.
- Development team for continuous product improvement and feature updates.
- Customer support team for maintaining high service quality.
- Marketing team for outreach and customer acquisition.

## Key Activities
- Continuous development and enhancement of the Mail-Tuner platform.
- Conducting market research to stay ahead of email deliverability trends.
- Building and maintaining partnerships with ESPs and other tech providers.
- Marketing and promotional activities to drive user acquisition.

## Key Partners
- Email service providers (ESPs) for integration and co-marketing opportunities.
- Digital marketing agencies for joint offerings and referrals.
- Cloud service providers for hosting and infrastructure support.
- Industry influencers and thought leaders for credibility and outreach.

## Cost Structure
- Development costs: salaries for engineers and data scientists.
- Marketing expenses: online advertising, content creation, and events.
- Customer support costs: salaries and tools for support staff.
- Operational costs: hosting services, software licenses, and infrastructure maintenance.
