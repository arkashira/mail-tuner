# Marketing Plan
## Positioning
Mail-tuner: AI-powered email deliverability and configuration management tool that maximizes business email potential.
Alternatives:
1. Email Optimization Platform
2. AI-driven Email Infrastructure Manager
3. Smart Email Deliverability Suite

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
1. **Emily Chen**, Marketing Manager at a mid-sized e-commerce company, responsible for email campaigns, with a budget of $10,000/month for marketing tools.
2. **David Lee**, IT Director at a large enterprise, overseeing email infrastructure, with a budget of $50,000/month for email management solutions.
3. **Rachel Patel**, Founder of a small startup, handling email marketing and operations, with a budget of $1,000/month for essential tools.

## Channels
1. **LinkedIn Ads** (CAC: $200): Targeted ads for marketing managers and IT directors.
2. **Google Ads** (CAC: $150): Search ads for businesses searching for email deliverability solutions.
3. **Email Marketing** (CAC: $50): Targeted campaigns to existing email lists and industry-specific newsletters.

## Content Cadence
Week 1-2: Introduction to email deliverability and configuration management.
Week 3-4: Case studies and success stories of businesses that improved email delivery rates.
Week 5-6: Product tutorials and feature highlights.
Week 7-8: Industry trends and best practices for email infrastructure optimization.
Week 9-10: Customer testimonials and user-generated content.
Week 11-12: Advanced features and upcoming product roadmap.

## Launch Milestones
* D-30: Finalize marketing materials, launch landing page, and start collecting interest.
* D-0: Official product launch, start paid advertising, and announce to existing network.
* D+30: Analyze early adoption metrics, adjust marketing strategy, and plan for content marketing.
* D+90: Review customer acquisition costs, retention rates, and revenue growth.

## Success Metrics
* DAU (Daily Active Users): 1,000 by D+90.
* $MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) target: $100,000 by D+90.
